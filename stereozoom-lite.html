STEREOZOOM LITE

Stereozoom Lite is an iOS app that lets you:

  • Open side-by-side (parallel or cross-view) and Spatial photos from Photos, Files, the share sheet, or the clipboard
  • View any compatible SBS 3D photo in SBS Parallel, SBS Cross, Grayscale Anaglyph, and Dubois Anaglyph mode
  • Zoom and pan with two fingers
  • Use automatic alignment, or align by hand with a one-finger swipe
  • Swap sides
  • Rotate 180°
  • Flip the image horizontally
  • Use Aspect Ratio and Fit & Align controls
  • Realign misaligned images
  • Apply image effects and hue/saturation/brightness adjustments

After you adjust a photo, you can save it as a side-by-side JPEG or PNG, a Spatial photo, a grayscale anaglyph, a Dubois anaglyph, or an animated wiggle GIF. Your alignment, effects and color adjustments are baked into the saved file, and you can crop it to the area you've zoomed in on.
